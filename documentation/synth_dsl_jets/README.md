# Synth DSL + analytic Taylor jets

This package implements a parameterized audio-synthesis DSL and an analytic Taylor-jet engine.

The local note model is represented by named nodes:

$$
f_0(t)=f_{\rm static}\left[1+q_v\sin(2\pi f_v(t-t_0)+\phi_v)\right]
$$

$$
\phi'(t)=2\pi f_0(t)
$$

with an analytically integrated phase for the small-vibrato model,

$$
\phi(t)=\phi_0+2\pi f_{\rm static}\tau +
\frac{f_{\rm static}q_v}{f_v}
\left[\cos\phi_v-\cos(2\pi f_v\tau+\phi_v)\right],
\qquad \tau=t-t_0.
$$

The smooth ADSR is a sum of Gaussian-CDF transitions. If

$$
G(u)=\frac12\left[1+\operatorname{erf}\left(\frac{4u}{\sqrt2}\right)\right],
$$

then

$$
E(t)=G(\tau/a)-(1-s)G((\tau-a)/d)-sG((\tau-T)/r).
$$

The factorized harmonic amplitude is

$$
A_k(t)=v\,E(t)\,H_k\,10^{S(f_k(t))/20},
$$

where

$$
f_k(t)=k f_0(t)\sqrt{1+B k^2}
$$

and the spectral envelope is an RBF interpolant in $\log_2 f$:

$$
S(f)=\frac{\sum_j S_j\exp\left[-\frac{(\log_2 f-u_j)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right]}{\sum_j\exp\left[-\frac{(\log_2 f-u_j)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right]}.
$$

The ordinary local source is then

$$
x_n(t)=\sum_{k=1}^{K} A_k(t)\sin\left(k\phi(t)+\psi_k\right)+x_{\rm noise}(t).
$$

## Jet representation

The Taylor jet stores

$$
J_k[f](t_0)=\frac{f^{(k)}(t_0)}{k!}.
$$

Thus the ordinary derivatives are recovered with

$$
f^{(k)}(t_0)=k!\,J_k[f](t_0).
$$

The implementation propagates jets analytically through $+$, $-$, $\times$, division, $\exp$, $\log$, $\sin$, $\cos$, $\sqrt{}$ and $\operatorname{erf}$. PyTorch autograd then differentiates the complete jet with respect to every free synthesis parameter.

## Outputs generated by the demo

- `demo.wav`: multivoice arpeggiated example.
- `demo_dsl.json`: symbolic named parameter DAG.
- `graph_spec.json`: causal graph summary.
- `ground_truth.json`: note events, timbre and parameter metadata.
- `analytic_jets.npz`: jets through fourth order.
- `jet_jacobian.npz`: Jacobian of all jet coefficients with respect to 31 parameters.
- `jet_symbolic_verification.csv`: independent SymPy verification.
- `jacobian_fd_check.npz`: finite-difference validation of the full Jacobian.

For nonlocal effects (delay, time-varying chorus and reverb), the package stores them as synthesis graph operations. They are not incorrectly represented as local scalar jets. A constant delay can nevertheless be handled analytically by the substitution $t\mapsto t-\tau$.
